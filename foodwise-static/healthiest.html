Healthiest items

Every canteen item, ranked by a simple nutrition score. A higher score means more protein for the calories and less fat. It is a quick guide, not a verdict: no food here is off limits, and items lower down are simply better enjoyed now and then.

    How the score works

    The score is out of 100 and uses only the numbers in our nutrition table:

    • Protein for the calories (50 points). 5 g of protein per 100 calories earns full marks.
    • Share of calories from fat (30 points). The lower the share, the more points. Items where fat supplies 45% or more of the calories earn none.
    • Lightness (20 points). Items up to 350 calories earn full marks, fading to zero at 500 calories.
    • Items with under 2 g of protein, such as cold drinks, have their score halved because their calories bring little nutrition.

    Vitamins, minerals, fibre and sugar are not part of the score, and all values are approximate. This is an awareness tool, not medical or dietary advice.
