FOOD PHOTOS
===========

Put one photo per dish in this folder, named exactly as below.
Accepted formats: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp  (any one of them is fine).
Until you add a photo, the site shows the drawing from images/illustrations/ for that dish, so every dish already has a picture.
Your photo always wins: as soon as images/foods/<name>.jpg exists, it replaces the drawing.

Tips
- Best: photograph the dishes at the canteen yourselves. Original photos suit a field project, and you avoid any copyright issues.
- Shoot landscape (wider than tall), in daylight, from slightly above. The site crops photos to 4:3 or square, so keep the dish in the middle.
- Keep each file under about 300 KB (resize to ~800 px wide) so the pages load quickly.
- If you use stock photos instead (for example from Unsplash or Pexels), check the licence and credit the photographer on the About page.
