Advantages of Canteen Food

Advantages of the canteen's food

The canteen menu provides a variety of nutrients and can be a convenient source of energy during the day. Items such as idli, dosa, poha, upma, sabudana khichdi and besan chilla provide carbohydrates for energy, while dal-based foods, sprouts, besan and peanuts provide plant-based protein.

Vegetables used in dishes such as fried rice, noodles, pasta, sandwiches, rolls and misal contribute vitamins, minerals, fibre and antioxidants. Items containing potatoes, rice, wheat and noodles help provide energy and keep students feeling full. Milk-based chai also provides some protein and calcium, while drinking water helps maintain hydration.

Overall, having a variety of foods available in the canteen lets students choose meals according to their nutritional needs, energy requirements and preferences.

General advantages of eating healthy food

Eating a healthy, balanced diet is important for overall health and well-being. Nutritious food provides the carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water the body needs for proper growth and daily activity.

A balanced diet helps maintain energy levels, supports the immune system, improves digestion, and supports healthy growth. Nutritious meals also support concentration, memory and productivity — particularly useful for students during classes and study.

Including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, pulses, nuts and enough water helps maintain balanced nutrition, while foods high in sugar, salt or unhealthy fat are best kept in moderation. Choosing a variety of nutritious foods in balanced portions is a key part of a healthy lifestyle.
