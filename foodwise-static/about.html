About FoodWise

FoodWise is a field project focused on college canteens, food awareness, healthy eating habits, students' food choices and nutritional information. The project aims to create awareness among students about the food they consume and help them make more informed food choices by providing information about ingredients, nutritional values, and the benefits of different food items.

Information for this project was gathered through both online (Google Form) and offline data collection — direct conversations with students and canteen staff. The project initially covered the MVLU College canteen, and Arnold College canteen was added afterward at our professor's request, so the site now compares food, pricing and nutrition across both campuses.
